§ 01What Chart Watcher is

Chart Watcher is a private, non-commercial tool that one family uses to keep up with one family member’s medical records. It is run by Austin Butler (Fat Tail Ads, LLC is listed as the developer of record with Epic). It is not offered to the public, not sold, and not affiliated with any hospital, health system, or Epic Systems.

By authorizing Chart Watcher in MyChart, you (the patient, or a proxy the patient has designated) agree to these terms.

§ 02Who may authorize it

Only the patient, or a person with proxy access the patient or their health system has granted, may authorize Chart Watcher. If you are not one of those people, choose “Deny access”.

§ 03What it reads, and what it never does

Chart Watcher only reads the categories you approve on the MyChart sharing screen — for example results, clinical notes, medications, conditions, allergies, visits and procedures, including records your health system holds from other organizations.

  • ◆It never writes to, changes, or deletes anything in the medical record.
  • ◆It never sends messages, requests refills, schedules, or takes any action in MyChart.
  • ◆It never sees or stores the MyChart password. Sign-in happens on the health system’s own page; Chart Watcher receives only the access token the health system issues.

§ 04How the data is kept

  • ◆Records are stored encrypted on the family’s own private server, which is not reachable from the public internet.
  • ◆Only the family member who runs it can access the data, from their own devices.
  • ◆Notifications sent to family members say only what kind of item is new (for example “2 new labs”) and contain no medical details.
  • ◆Server logs record counts and record identifiers only, never record contents.

§ 05What it never does with the data

  • ◆No selling, renting, or sharing of the data with anyone, ever.
  • ◆No advertising, marketing, profiling, or analytics.
  • ◆No use for any purpose other than helping the family follow the patient’s care.

The family may use an AI assistant they control to help them read and understand the records. That is part of the family’s own use, not a sharing of the data with a third party for its own purposes.

§ 06How long access lasts, and how to stop it

Access lasts for the period you choose on the MyChart sharing screen. You can end it at any time in MyChart under the account’s linked apps or “Manage my linked apps and devices” settings; Chart Watcher stops receiving data immediately.

On request, or if access is revoked and not renewed, the family will delete the stored copy of the records.

§ 07Not medical advice

Chart Watcher only organizes and displays information already in the patient’s record. It does not diagnose, interpret, or give medical advice. Questions about care belong with the patient’s clinicians.

§ 08No warranty

Chart Watcher is provided as-is for personal family use. Data may be delayed or incomplete compared with what MyChart shows (some inpatient notes and flowsheets are not available through this connection). Do not rely on it for urgent or time-sensitive decisions.
